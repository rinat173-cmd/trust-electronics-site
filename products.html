Sensing

Lidar

Cost-effective, scalable, versatile sensing platforms that address perception & Lidar development needs from ADAS to AD. Sensor-fusion and perception generate accurate 3D environmental models supporting all SAE autonomy levels via AI and computer-vision algorithms. Beam-width options 19°, 36° and 48°; max sensing distance 165 m; 16 detection segments; real-time acquisition and display (USB); RS-485 and CAN bus; interfaces include DIP switches (4), MicroSD slot and an expansion connector (UART, CAN, SPI, GPIO, DAC).

Identification

RFID

Trust Electronics is a large supplier of RFID hardware with many years of experience — RFID tags and labels, smartcards, readers and application-system solutions. Applications include laundry, smart-house access control, security and fuel systems. The range covers smart labels, ISO/clamshell cards, ABS & PVC key and disc tags, wrist bands, HF/UHF labels and hard tags, poultry ring tags, epoxy tags and more. We work with major semiconductor manufacturers and design houses, with R&D as a strategic priority.

Connectivity

BG930 — LTE IoT Gateway Module

A gateway module for data communication across platforms — collecting information from the field and transmitting it over the internet and the cellular network to a cloud or a local server. Supports 2G, LTE CAT-M1/NB2 and EGPRS cellular standards, integrated with GNSS positioning.

Lighting

LED Chips & Lighting Solutions

We offer world-top LEDs — the widest spectrum of LED emitters in the industry, from UV to IR, ranging from 200 nm to 1650 nm. Featured are SunLike LEDs, which reproduce the sunlight spectrum for indoor environments: high-voltage LED for simple use, optimized optics, and reduced excessive blue-light wavelengths — because the light we feel most comfortable with is natural light. As an authorized representative / distributor we supply LED lighting components including SunLike and custom drivers.
