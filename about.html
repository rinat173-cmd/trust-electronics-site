Company Profile

Our profile

Trust Electronics Ltd. is an established Israeli company with more than 30 years of experience in supplying electronic components, sub-assemblies, technologies, and engineering solutions to the local market.

Founded in 1990, Trust Electronics is ISO 9001 certified and represents leading international manufacturers and suppliers in Israel. We maintain close, long-term relationships with both our customers and our global technology partners, supporting products from initial design-in through production.

Our areas of expertise

  • Passive electronic components
  • RF and microwave components
  • High-power LEDs and laser technologies
  • RFID and wireless solutions
  • Electronic components and sub-assemblies
  • Component engineering and technical support
  • Turnkey electronic solutions and subsystem development

Our turnkey capabilities include electronic subsystem development, PCB design, component selection, and component-engineering services, allowing us to provide customers with complete technical solutions in addition to component supply.

Trust Electronics has extensive access to the Israeli electronics industry and works with a broad range of customers, including international companies with Israeli design and R&D activities, high-tech companies, research institutes, and organizations involved in AI, Robotics, consumer, automotive, medical, military, and space applications.

Our team consists of highly skilled and experienced engineers who are familiar with the requirements and decision-making processes of the Israeli electronics industry. This technical expertise, combined with strong customer relationships and a high level of personal commitment, enables us to successfully introduce new technologies, support design-in activities, and develop long-term business opportunities for our represented manufacturers.

Our main office and warehouse are located near Israel's international airport, providing convenient access for both customers and international suppliers. Our warehouse infrastructure enables us to support annual supply programs as well as urgent customer requirements.

Trust Electronics' strategy is based on technical expertise, close customer relationships, professional service, and long-term cooperation with leading international technology companies. We are committed to developing the Israeli market for our partners and creating sustainable business opportunities through active sales, engineering support, and customer engagement.
