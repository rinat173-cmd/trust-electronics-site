Accessibility Statement

We are committed to making this website usable for everyone, including people with disabilities.

Statement published: 2026 · Last reviewed: 2026

Please note: This statement is a template reflecting the measures implemented on this site. Before publishing, confirm the details (coordinator name, dates, exceptions) and have it reviewed to ensure it meets the Israeli accessibility regulations for your business.

Our commitment

Trust Electronics Ltd views equal, respectful and accessible service as a core value. We invest ongoing effort to make our website accessible to people with disabilities, in the belief that everyone has the right to use digital services with dignity and independence.

Applicable standard

This website has been made accessible in line with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations (Service Accessibility Adjustments), 2013, and the Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on the international WCAG 2.0 guidelines at Level AA.

Accessibility features on this site

  • An on-site accessibility toolbar (button at the bottom-left of every page) offering: larger / smaller text, high-contrast mode, link highlighting, a readable font, and grayscale.
  • Semantic HTML structure with proper headings and landmarks for screen readers.
  • Full keyboard navigation, including a visible focus indicator and a "skip to main content" link.
  • Descriptive text alternatives for meaningful images and icons; decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technology.
  • A responsive layout that adapts to different screen sizes and supports browser zoom.
  • Respect for the operating-system "reduce motion" preference to minimize animation for sensitive users.

Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some content may not yet be fully accessible. We are working continuously to improve. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please let us know using the details below and we will do our best to resolve it.
