Design & production services

From the first concept of a design, through PCB layout, to production in-house — plus analysis, mechanical and QA engineering.

Software Design

Leading-edge FPGA design — planning and implementing highly advanced designs and algorithms across DSP, communication and RF, high-speed serial protocols (integrated FPGA transceivers) and analog front-end (A2D/D2A, DDC/DUC, digital filters). We are using AI and also develop web & app software — browser extensions, data-harvesting tools, iOS/Android apps and telephony systems (HTML5, JavaScript, C++, Java, PHP, React Native, Django, ASP.NET).

Hardware Design

Board design covering digital design with in-house PCB layout and prototypes, mixed-signal design with analysis, small-footprint, high-power and battery-operated designs, and RF & wireless. PCB layout includes library management, signal & power integrity, DFx (design for testing / manufacture / assembly) and manufacturing support.

Turn-key Projects

Our Turn-Key division provides engineering design services from the first concept of design via PCB layout and production, with QA and engineering solutions and meticulous testing of every batch before shipment. We lead complex end-to-end projects with rich experience in hardware, firmware, embedded, software, web & app — a true partnership from start to completion.

ASIC — Software & Hardware

Turnkey ASIC solutions for analog, mixed-signal and RF silicon IPs in the 800 MHz–18 GHz range — for radars, satellite systems, backhaul and 5G communication. An extensive IP portfolio brings products to market at lower cost, risk and time. We represent the RWMMIC, supporting high-performance Gallium Arsenide MMICs.

Mechanical Design & Engineering

An integrated suite for developing 3D digital twins in a flexible product-design environment — enclosure design and feasibility studies, 3D CAD modeling, and integration of electronic design, PCB layout and industrial design, through prototype and manufacturing support.

RF & Wireless Design

RF & microwave engineering experienced in the design of TX/RX chains, VCOs and PLL synthesizers, up/down converter modules and satellite radio up to 31 GHz. Specialties: wideband TX/RX design up to 41 GHz, ultra-low phase-noise wideband synthesizers & VCO, multi-loop PLL synthesizers, and vast analog & DC design experience.

NPI Service

New Product Introduction — the structured process of transitioning a product from development to production and to market. Through planning, collaboration and oversight we reduce risk and keep budget and schedule aligned: team formation, supplier selection, development, prototype, quality testing and assembly documentation.

Analysis & QA Engineering

Analysis services and quality-assurance engineering — signal & power integrity, 3D PCB simulation, thermal and dynamic analysis — with meticulous testing of every batch before shipment.

In-house Production

Production kept in-house alongside design, so issues are caught and closed fast — without external handoffs.

How we work

A clear path from idea to shipment

  • Concept & feasibility — scope the design and de-risk it early.
  • Design — hardware, software, RF and mechanical in one team.
  • Prototype & NPI — validate, then prepare for volume.
  • Production & QA — build in-house and test every batch.
